# Century Blog Publishing OS

Primary domain: https://www.centuryblog.com.ng

## Objective

Century Blog Publishing OS is the operating model for creating, reviewing, publishing, updating, and refreshing content without breaking SEO, trust, or production workflows.

## Content lanes

- Breaking news
- Live updates
- Explainers
- Evergreen authority articles
- Fact checks
- Data stories
- Service journalism

## Workflow map

1. Topic intake
2. Assignment and angle definition
3. Source collection
4. Drafting in the correct template
5. Editorial QA
6. Metadata and internal linking pass
7. Publication
8. Distribution and refresh review

## Intake rules

Every topic proposal should answer:

- Why now?
- Why Century Blog?
- Who is the audience?
- What question does the story answer?
- What reliable sources are available?

## Drafting standards

- Use the appropriate template from `/content/templates/`
- Match category carefully
- Add at least one internal link target before final sign-off
- Add source notes for sensitive topics
- Keep intros concrete and useful

## Review states

- Draft
- Pending review
- Approved
- Published
- Rejected
- Scheduled

## Publication safety rules

- Never publish unsupported allegations
- Never publish fake citations
- Never rely on screenshots as sole evidence
- Never treat source gaps as minor on sensitive topics
- Never publish evergreen guides with thin or generic advice

## SEO operations

- Canonical, Open Graph, Twitter, and schema should all resolve to `https://www.centuryblog.com.ng`
- Published articles should be indexable unless deliberately noindexed
- Sitemap and robots must remain healthy
- Category pages must stay relevant to their assigned content

## Internal linking workflow

During drafting:

- Add the category page
- Add one related recent article
- Add one relevant evergreen piece or explainer

During QA:

- Check that anchor text sounds natural
- Remove repetitive or forced anchors

## Quality gate questions

- Is this useful enough to earn organic traffic?
- Is this different from a basic rewrite elsewhere?
- Does the story explain why the reader should care?
- Is there clear attribution?
- Would this improve or weaken AdSense readiness?

## Refresh system

- News updates: review same day if a material development occurs
- Explainers and evergreen posts: queue refresh every quarter
- Data and policy posts: refresh when new data is released
- Fact checks: update when new evidence emerges

## Team roles

- Executive editor: assigns and approves major stories
- Standards editor: checks sourcing, fairness, and policy risk
- SEO editor: checks metadata, search intent, and internal links
- Copy editor: clarity, grammar, and consistency
- Publisher: final checks and release timing

## Master publishing prompt

> Build a Century Blog article that is calm, factual, original in structure, and genuinely helpful. Use a reader-first lead, explain context, attribute claims, include Nigeria relevance where useful, avoid fake precision, and finish with a useful takeaway.

## Failure states to avoid

- Thin content
- Category mismatch
- Duplicate angle
- Over-optimised keyword repetition
- Placeholder language on public pages
- Missing trust signals
- Publishing without image alt text
- Publishing without metadata

## Daily publishing OS checklist

1. Review the content calendar
2. Prioritise one high-trust authority piece
3. Fill breaking-news gaps only where value exists
4. Refresh at least one older post if needed
5. Check analytics and reader response
6. Revisit sensitive stories for corrections or updates
