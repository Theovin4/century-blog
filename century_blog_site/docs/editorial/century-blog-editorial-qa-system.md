# Century Blog Editorial QA System

Primary domain: https://www.centuryblog.com.ng

## Purpose

This QA system exists to stop weak, shallow, repetitive, or unsafe content from reaching publication. It applies to manual drafts, edited auto drafts, evergreen explainers, fact checks, and data stories.

## QA review layers

### Layer 1: Reporter self-check

- Is the angle clear?
- Is the lead useful?
- Are key facts sourced?
- Is the article original in framing and structure?
- Are internal links included?

### Layer 2: Section editor check

- Headline quality
- Structural clarity
- Tone and neutrality
- Relevance to category
- SEO completeness

### Layer 3: Standards/editorial QA

- Source credibility
- Statistical accuracy
- Legal sensitivity
- AdSense readiness
- Discover readiness

## Editorial QA scorecard

Score 0 to 2 for each area:

- Headline clarity
- Reader value
- Fact verification
- Source quality
- Originality
- Structure
- SEO completeness
- Internal linking
- Image readiness
- Trust/policy safety

Maximum score: 20

Decision thresholds:

- 18-20: approve
- 15-17: approve with minor changes
- 11-14: major revision
- 0-10: reject

## Fact-checking rules

- Verify every specific figure, date, and title.
- If a figure cannot be confirmed, remove it or reframe it.
- Attribute claims inside the body, not just at the end.
- Prefer the most recent source unless historical context is required.
- If facts are still moving, say so clearly.

## Statistics QA checklist

- Is the source named?
- Is the year or reporting period clear?
- Is the statistic used in the correct context?
- Does newer data exist?
- Does the number need comparison to mean anything useful?

## Source standards

High-confidence source tiers:

1. Official records and public institutions
2. Multilateral agencies and peer-reviewed research
3. Public company or institution statements
4. Reputable wire services and major newsrooms

Low-confidence source types needing escalation:

- Viral posts
- Anonymous screenshots
- Anonymous WhatsApp claims
- Unverified blog summaries
- Recycled aggregators citing each other

## AI-pattern detection checklist

Flag and revise if the article:

- Repeats the same sentence shape
- Uses vague transitions too often
- Sounds symmetrical section by section
- Makes generic claims without evidence
- Uses filler such as "in today's fast-paced world"
- Provides fake certainty or unsupported precision

## AdSense QA checklist

- Does the article provide enough original value?
- Is the page more useful than a quick summary elsewhere?
- Is the article free of copied passages?
- Is any sensitive advice framed carefully?
- Are trust pages reachable from the public site?
- Does the page avoid clutter and low-value modules?

## Legal and risk flags

Escalate for higher review if the draft includes:

- Allegations about a named person
- Crime or court claims
- Health advice or treatment implications
- Financial or investment recommendations
- Suicide, self-harm, war, mass casualty, or sensitive violence

## Editorial QA prompt

> Review this Century Blog draft like a major newsroom editor. Be strict on accuracy, sourcing, originality, SEO usefulness, structure, and AdSense safety. Reject vague claims, AI-sounding filler, weak headings, and unsupported numbers. Recommend precise fixes, not general praise.

## Publication sign-off requirements

Before approval, confirm:

- Metadata is complete
- Canonical uses `https://www.centuryblog.com.ng`
- Internal links are present
- Trust/risk language is appropriate
- Hero image and alt text are ready
- Category is correct

## Post-publication QA

Within the first review window, confirm:

- Headline renders correctly on page and cards
- Article is in sitemap if published
- Schema renders without obvious data gaps
- Links work
- Images load on mobile and desktop
