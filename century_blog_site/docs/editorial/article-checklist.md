# Century Blog Article Checklist

Primary domain: https://www.centuryblog.com.ng

## Before drafting

- Topic is relevant and worth publishing
- Category is correct
- Reliable sources are available
- Search intent is clear

## Before editor review

- Headline is clear and accurate
- Intro explains why readers should care
- Facts are attributed
- Statistics are current and contextualised
- Structure is easy to follow
- Nigeria or Africa relevance is included where useful
- Internal links are added
- External sources are authoritative

## Before publication

- SEO title is ready
- Meta title is ready
- Meta description is ready
- Slug is clean
- Canonical uses `https://www.centuryblog.com.ng`
- Hero image is approved
- Alt text is written
- Trust-sensitive wording is checked
- AdSense and low-value risks are reviewed

## Final newsroom questions

- Would a reader feel more informed after reading this?
- Does the article offer something beyond a simple summary?
- Is any claim overstated?
- Is any paragraph filler?
- Would you be comfortable putting this on the homepage?
