# Century Blog Editorial System

Primary domain: https://www.centuryblog.com.ng

## Editorial mission

Century Blog exists to help readers in Nigeria and beyond understand important stories quickly, accurately, and with enough context to make the information useful. The publication should feel calm, trustworthy, and practical rather than noisy, sensational, or copied.

## Editorial voice

- Clear, direct, and human.
- Calm under pressure during breaking news.
- Explanatory when stories are complex.
- Neutral in tone, especially for politics, conflict, health, finance, and crime.
- Reader-first: always answer "why this matters" early.
- British English by default.

## Core writing formula

1. Lead with the core development and why it matters.
2. Add context readers need to understand the story.
3. Explain impact on people, institutions, markets, or public life.
4. Include Nigeria or Africa relevance where appropriate.
5. Attribute important facts to reliable sources.
6. End with what to watch next.

## Required article elements

- SEO title
- Meta title
- Meta description
- Canonical URL using `https://www.centuryblog.com.ng`
- Category and tags
- Author name
- Published date
- Updated date when relevant
- Hero image recommendation or approved asset
- Image alt text
- Executive summary
- Clear heading structure
- Internal links
- External source list where appropriate
- Conclusion

## E-E-A-T checklist

- Experience: does the article reflect practical understanding or reporting context?
- Expertise: are claims framed carefully and supported by credible sources?
- Authoritativeness: does the piece connect to strong source material and Century Blog's existing coverage?
- Trustworthiness: is the tone neutral, transparent, and free from exaggeration?

## Source standards

Use primary sources first where possible:

- Government departments and regulators
- Court filings and official legal records
- Central banks and statistical agencies
- WHO, UN agencies, World Bank, IMF, OECD
- Universities and peer-reviewed research
- Public company statements and filings
- Reputable news agencies for breaking-news confirmation

Avoid:

- Unsourced social media claims
- Low-trust blogs
- Rumour accounts
- Screenshots without verification
- Anonymous claims that cannot be justified editorially

## Fact-checking workflow

1. Confirm the basic event, claim, or development.
2. Verify names, titles, dates, locations, and numbers.
3. Check whether any statistics are current.
4. Look for conflicting reports before publication.
5. Add attribution inside the article body, not only in metadata.
6. Flag uncertain claims with cautious wording.

## Image standards

- Use licensed, owned, public-domain, or approved editorial imagery only.
- Do not use copied celebrity or press photography without rights.
- Every image needs alt text that describes what is shown.
- Hero images must match the story subject, not a generic mood image unless clearly labelled as illustration.
- Charts or data visuals should cite the dataset source in the caption or body.

## Internal linking strategy

Each substantial article should include:

- 1 link to the relevant category page
- 1 link to a related recent story
- 1 link to a relevant evergreen explainer or guide
- Optional links to trust pages when context requires it

Link naturally inside sentences. Avoid link stuffing or repetitive anchor text.

## SEO checklist

- Main keyword appears naturally in title, intro, H2s, and meta description.
- Slug is short, readable, and aligned with the article focus.
- Meta title stays within display limits.
- Meta description is specific and human.
- Headings reflect reader intent, not keyword stuffing.
- Related internal links are added.
- Schema type matches the content type.

## Google Discover checklist

- Strong, specific headline
- Real story value or helpful analysis
- High-quality hero image
- No misleading promise or clickbait framing
- Fresh angle or original explanation
- Clean metadata and canonical

## AdSense checklist

- No thin or doorway content
- No fake expertise
- No copied passages
- No misleading health or financial advice
- No sensational or manipulative language
- Public trust pages must remain reachable
- Article should provide standalone reader value

## Legal and policy review checklist

- Check for defamation risk
- Avoid stating allegations as facts
- Avoid medical or financial prescriptions without proper framing
- Check copyright status of images and quoted material
- Add disclaimers where the topic is sensitive

## Content refresh system

- Breaking news: review within 24 hours if the story evolves
- Evergreen explainers: review every 90 to 180 days
- High-traffic finance, health, and policy pages: review monthly
- Update outdated facts, stats, screenshots, and links

## Quality scoring system

Score each draft from 1 to 5 in:

- Accuracy
- Source quality
- Clarity
- Originality
- Search usefulness
- Trust/AdSense safety

Publication target:

- Breaking news: minimum 20/30
- Authority evergreen: minimum 24/30

## Master article generation prompt

Use this internally when shaping new articles:

> Write for Century Blog, a premium Nigeria-first digital publication. Use British English, clear structure, strong factual caution, and practical reader relevance. Open with why the story matters, add verified context, explain impact, include Nigeria relevance where useful, attribute key facts, and end with what to watch next. Avoid filler, hype, fake precision, and robotic transitions.

## Publishing workflow summary

1. Pitch or source selection
2. Source gathering
3. Drafting in approved template
4. Fact-check and link review
5. SEO and metadata review
6. Legal/risk pass where needed
7. Publish
8. Recheck sitemap, rendering, and headline presentation
