# Citation Templates

## Inline attribution examples

- According to the Central Bank of Nigeria, ...
- The World Health Organization said in its latest guidance that ...
- A Reuters report published on [date] said ...
- Court documents reviewed by Century Blog show ...

## Source note formats

### Official document

- Source name:
- Document title:
- Publication date:
- URL:
- Why it matters:

### Dataset

- Dataset owner:
- Dataset title:
- Date range:
- Access date:
- URL:

### Research paper

- Authors:
- Journal or institution:
- Publication year:
- Link:
- Key finding used:

### Company announcement

- Company:
- Statement type:
- Date:
- Link:
- Claim used:

## Rules

- Name the source clearly in the body when it carries a key fact.
- Add the year or reporting period for statistics.
- Do not cite another article that merely cites an unavailable source if the original can be found.

## Update frequency

- Review every quarter or when new coverage needs arise
