# Source Library

## Purpose

This file tracks preferred source types and reliable starting points by coverage area.

## Source tiers

### Tier 1: Primary

- Official government releases
- Court records
- Regulatory filings
- Public company filings
- Official datasets
- Peer-reviewed journals

### Tier 2: Strong secondary

- Reuters
- AP
- AFP
- BBC
- Financial Times
- WSJ
- Major national outlets with strong correction records

### Tier 3: Use cautiously

- Expert commentary
- Think-tank analysis
- Industry association reports

### Avoid unless independently verified

- Anonymous social posts
- Tabloid-style aggregation
- Viral screenshots
- Low-quality SEO blogs

## Beat-based source notes

- Nigeria politics: official statements, gazettes, court records
- Business: regulators, filings, central banks, investor updates
- Health: WHO, NCDC, peer-reviewed research, major journals
- Technology: company docs, security advisories, regulator guidance
- World: official foreign offices, multilateral agencies, strong wire services

## Update frequency

- Review quarterly
- Add new sources only after standards review
