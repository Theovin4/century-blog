# Search / AdSense baseline — Phase 1

**Verified in source code:** canonical URL default, page-level metadata, robots route, XML and news sitemap routes, basic JSON-LD, `ads.txt`, AdSense publisher metadata, editorial trust pages, article pages.

**Risk:** content quality gating incorrectly treats word count and prescribed headings as publication/index eligibility. Content templates may encourage boilerplate. News sitemap requires >=1600 words by code. These should be changed with editorial review and integration tests.

**Unverified live:** sitemap response and acceptance, Search Console statistics, live page count, real Article structured data validation, AdSense policy diagnostics, Discover traffic, CWV field data.

**AdSense priority:** original verified reporting and value first; review source attribution, author identities, duplication, navigation, trust pages and accurate disclosures. AdSense approval and search rank cannot be guaranteed.
